# How Much Are Solar Panels in Maine?

How much are solar panels in Maine? Most Maine homeowners pay based on system size, equipment, roof layout, and incentives. Installed prices are often quoted around $2.50 to $3.50 per watt before incentives, so a typical 6 kW system might cost roughly $15,000 to $21,000 gross, while larger 8-10 kW systems cost more upfront and offset more electricity.

## What affects the price?

- System size and panel efficiency
- Inverter type and roof complexity
- Snow load, winter production, and roof condition in Maine
- Federal, state, and utility incentives
- Net metering rules and your local electric rate
